Need Immediate Mental Health Support?

If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, thoughts of self-harm, or feel that you are in immediate danger, please seek emergency assistance without waiting for a consultation appointment.

  • Contact your local emergency services immediately.
  • Reach out to a trusted family member or close friend.
  • Visit the nearest hospital or emergency department.
  • Contact your local mental health crisis helpline.
