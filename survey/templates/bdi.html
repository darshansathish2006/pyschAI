Beck Depression Inventory (BDI-II)

A widely used self-report questionnaire designed to measure the severity of depressive symptoms.

About BDI

The Beck Depression Inventory consists of 21 questions that help evaluate emotional, cognitive, and physical symptoms associated with depression.

Instructions

  • Answer every question honestly.
  • Select the option that best describes you.
  • Consider how you have felt during the past two weeks.
  • Estimated time: 5–10 minutes.
Disclaimer: This assessment is intended for educational and self-reflection purposes only. It should not be used as a substitute for diagnosis or treatment by a qualified mental health professional.
